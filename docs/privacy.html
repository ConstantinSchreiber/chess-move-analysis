Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Best Move does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data.

Photos and camera

Images you take or choose are processed entirely on your device to recognise the chess position. They are never uploaded or shared, and the app does not keep them after the scan.

Analysis

The Stockfish chess engine runs on your device. No network connection is needed.

Recent positions

Positions you analysed are saved on your device (as text) so you can reopen them. You can clear them from the home screen (Recent positions → ⋯ → Clear history); deleting the app removes them too.

No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking

The app has no sign-in and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Network access

The app only connects to the internet when you choose “Open on lichess.org”, which opens that website in your browser. Lichess’s own privacy policy applies there.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
