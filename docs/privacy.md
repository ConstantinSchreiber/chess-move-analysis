# Tempo — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Tempo does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data.

- **Photos and camera.** Images you capture or choose are processed entirely on your device to
  recognise the chess position. They are never uploaded.
- **Analysis.** The Stockfish chess engine runs on your device. No network connection is needed.
- **History.** Recently analysed positions (as FEN text) are stored locally on your device so you
  can reopen them. Deleting the app removes them.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.**

The only time Tempo touches the network is when you explicitly choose "Open on lichess.org",
which opens that website in your browser.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ConstantinSchreiber/chess-move-analysis
